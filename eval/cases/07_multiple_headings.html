Content review

Editorial checklist

Good website reviews start with the needs of visitors. A useful page explains its subject clearly, answers practical questions, and helps a reader decide what to do next. Editors can organize related ideas under descriptive headings and provide links with meaningful labels. Images should support the explanation and include alternatives when they communicate information. Consistent page metadata makes content easier to identify when shared. These examples demonstrate an editorial review process rather than a promise about search rankings or traffic. Every recommendation needs context and human judgment before publication.
